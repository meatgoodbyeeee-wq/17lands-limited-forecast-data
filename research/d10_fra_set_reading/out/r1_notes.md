# r1 notes — FRA two-colour pair judgement

## Format speed
- Medium speed, leaning grindy: commons carry lots of cheap removal (B: #163, #34, #109; R: #203, #112, #38; W: #223), plus five basic-landcyclers and many "empower Jace" value cards that generate card advantage.
- Few cheap evasive aggro threats at common; 2-for-1 creatures (#162, #40, #124, #13) and removal density should reward removal-heavy midrange over go-wide aggro or slow ramp.

## BR (rank 1, 7.8)
- The two deepest removal colours: B commons #163, #34, #109 and R commons #203, #112, #38, plus uncommons #17, #39, #126, #175.
- Gold common #178 (3/3 that flies/hastes after noncombat ping) and Vicious Verse preps (#165, #259) make the ping theme easy to enable.
- Signposts #79, #210 (mythic), #115 reward the plan; solid curve from #35, #41, #182, #164.

## WB (rank 2, 7.3)
- White's best commons (O-ring #223, Angel #13, Pegasus #105) pair with black's removal suite.
- Uncommon removal #180 and #51 are premium; #46 and #158 give a sticky sacrifice/lifegain shell.
- Fliers (#13, #105, #122) close games in a removal-heavy, board-stalled format.

## UB (rank 3, 7.0)
- Blue's high-quality value commons (#162 3/4+Cadet, #29, #31, #161 lockdown aura) plus black removal.
- Gold commons #49 (threshold flier) and uncommons #176, #48, #256 support a mill/threshold control plan with fliers.
- Weak point: blue's 2-drops are thin, but the format pace should allow it.

## WR (rank 4, 6.6)
- Aggro/counters deck: red removal plus W O-ring and #180; #241 is a flexible common trick.
- Signposts #104 (counters on entering creatures) and #184 (removal + equipment) are strong.
- Cheap bodies #198, #67, #41, #182; risk is low creature quality vs. 2-for-1 midrange boards.

## BG (rank 5, 6.3)
- Black removal carries it; green adds solid bodies (#114 5/5 ward, #124 4/4 flashback, #183).
- Gold #22 (recursive deathtouch aura), #23, #103 (graveyard-scaled -1/-1) are good; #237 fuels graveyard.
- Lacks evasion beyond menace; strategy is grind-and-outsize.

## UR (rank 6, 6.2)
- Spells/prowess: red removal + blue tempo, #50 flashback cantrip, #208, #12 thopter maker, #72.
- Prowess commons (#68, #111) are low-impact bodies; deck depends on drawing enough removal.
- Good card flow, but creature quality is thin at common.

## WU (rank 7, 6.0)
- Surveil/fliers: #181, #162 (hybrid W/U prep), #21, #257, #171, mythic #209.
- Plenty of fliers but little removal outside W (#223, #180, #274) and U's #161.
- Cadet token generation is good value but the deck lacks punch against black/red removal.

## RG (rank 8, 5.7)
- Heartwood/ramp into fat: #47, #264 prep, #78 7/5 finisher, #172 flexible instant, mythic #206.
- Green's lone common removal (#242 bite) is helped by red burn, but ramp is slow in a removal-rich format.
- Big bodies (#114, #42, #124) are solid; few fliers and reach-only answers to air.

## WG (rank 9, 5.0)
- +1/+1 counters / lifegain (#170, #263, #20, #177, #142) — mostly small incremental effects.
- Neither colour has much common removal (W: #223, #106; G: #242); low-power cards like #66 and #185 fill slots.
- Bodies okay but the plan is weaker than removal-backed decks.

## UG (rank 10, 4.6)
- Signposts (#238, #159, #4, #27, #207) lean on actual planeswalkers/Jace loyalty — mostly parasitic or rare-dependent.
- Fewest removal options of any pair (#161, #242, #96 fight, #224 soft).
- Good bodies (#162, #114, #124, #31) keep it playable but it's outclassed by black/red decks.
