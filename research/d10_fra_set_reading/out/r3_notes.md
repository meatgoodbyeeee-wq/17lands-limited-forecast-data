# r3 notes — FRA two-colour pair judgement

## Format speed
- Medium-slow. Every colour has cheap common removal (B: -3/-3, 4-MV exile, kill-with-cost; R: 3 dmg, 4 dmg, 6 dmg; W: O-ring), plus landcycling 5-6 drops and Jace-token card advantage (surveil/draw), so games should go long and grind.
- Removal density and two-for-ones should beat pure aggro; evasive fliers and value bodies (prepared creatures, flashback Beast, Cadet makers) are the main win routes.

## BR — 7.5 (rank 1)
- The two deepest removal colours at common: #163, #34, #109, #203, #112, #38, plus uncommons #17, #39, #126, #175.
- Gold cards line up: Vicious Verse pingers (#178 3/3 that gains flying+haste, #259), #79, #175 modal shock; noncombat-damage theme is easy to enable.
- Creature quality is fine (#42, #40, #164), and red 2-drops cover the curve.

## WB — 7.0 (rank 2)
- Black removal plus white's O-ring common (#223), #106, uncommon #180/#274; #51 gold uncommon is premium removal.
- White fliers (#13, #105) and black #122 give evasion; #46 recursive lifelinker and #158 are decent glue.
- Lifegain/Cadet tokens are light synergy, but raw card quality carries it.

## BG — 6.6 (rank 3)
- Black removal with green's best bodies (#114 5/5 ward, #124 flashback 4/4, #183) and bite #242.
- Graveyard/lands theme (#23, #103, #237, threshold cards) adds value in a grindy format.
- #22 gold common is mediocre; few fliers, so relies on removal to handle air.

## RG — 6.3 (rank 4)
- Red common removal plus green bodies; #78 7/5 two-mode uncommon and #172 modal (6 to a flier) are strong.
- Heartwood ramp (#47, #264, #168) helps cast big things; #44 and #172 answer fliers.
- Light on evasion and card advantage compared with black decks.

## WR — 6.1 (rank 5)
- Red burn plus white O-ring/fliers; #184 equipment (3 dmg + 3 life) and #104 Mabel counters are good uncommons.
- Aggressive go-wide plan (#241, #198, #41) is weaker in a removal-heavy, slower format.
- Gold common #241 is just a trick; deck quality depends on mono-colour commons.

## UB — 6.0 (rank 6)
- Black removal plus blue value (#162, #30, #232 Jace) and solid fliers (#31 serpent, #49 threshold flier).
- Mill/threshold theme (#37, #49, #256, #48) is coherent; #176 modal is flexible.
- Slow and low-pressure; may lose to faster removal decks, but grinds well.

## UR — 5.7 (rank 7)
- Red removal is the backbone; blue adds card draw, counters (#224), tempo (#32, #29).
- Spells/prowess/thopter payoff (#12, #59, #111) needs noncreature density that commons only partly supply.
- #50 gold common is low impact; creature quality thin.

## WU — 5.6 (rank 8)
- Surveil/flier deck: #21 3/4 flier, #181, #257, #209 mythic; fliers at common (#13, #105, #31, #108).
- Removal limited to O-ring (#223), pacifism aura (#161), #106; relies on evasion races.
- Jace empower cards give card flow, but lacks hard answers to bombs.

## WG — 5.2 (rank 9)
- +1/+1 counters / lifegain (#170, #263, #20, #67, #66) is coherent but low power.
- Removal: O-ring, bite #242, #106, #177 fight; green fat plus white fliers is OK.
- Small, durdly payoffs; few ways to break stalls beyond fliers.

## UG — 4.9 (rank 10)
- Jace/planeswalker theme (#238, #207, #159, #204) is fiddly; payoffs mostly at rare.
- Least removal of any pair (#161 aura, #242 bite, #96 fight uncommon).
- Neither colour has efficient aggressive bodies, so it gets out-valued by black and out-tempoed by red.
