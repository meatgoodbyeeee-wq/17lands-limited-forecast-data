# r2 notes — FRA two-colour pair judgement

## Format speed
- Removal is plentiful at common (B: #34, #109, #163; R: #112, #203, #38; W: #223, #106; U: #161; G: #242), so go-wide/counter synergy decks get picked apart and 2-for-1 value matters.
- Prepare creatures, Jace-token "empower" cards and landcycling 5-6 drops give lots of card advantage; expect a medium-speed, removal-driven midrange format where cheap aggro is held back by 2/3 and 1/4 blockers.
- Evasion (white/blue fliers, black menace) and noncombat reach (BR pings, 3-dmg burn to face) decide stalled boards.

## BR — rank 1 (7.6)
- Deepest removal pairing: #163, #34, #109 + #112, #203, #38 at common; #39, #126, #89, #121, #175 at uncommon.
- Signpost commons/uncommons are on-plan: #178 (3/3 that flies/hastes after a free Vicious Verse ping), #259, #79, #175 charm.
- Ping/noncombat-damage theme converts stalled boards; black's #122 drain flier and #33 menace finisher close games.
- Weakness: few cheap evasive threats, but removal density should carry it.

## WB — rank 2 (7.3)
- White has the best common removal-ish card (#223 O-ring) plus #106, #180, #274; black adds three removal commons.
- Strong fliers (#13, #105, #122) plus #51 removal sorcery, #143 rare.
- Gold common #46 is weak, and the lifegain/sacrifice payoff is thin, but raw card quality is high.
- Good midrange control: kill everything, win in the air.

## WR — rank 3 (6.9)
- White fliers + red burn; #184 (Shock-plus-lifegain equipment) and #104 Mabel are solid signposts.
- Counters/tokens aggro (#15, #66, #40, #241 trick) fits a red curve with #41, #198, #111.
- Gold common #241 is only a trick; deck can be outvalued in long games against black.

## UB — rank 4 (6.7)
- Black removal plus blue's #161 lockdown aura and #224; blue value commons (#162, #30, #232, #16).
- Mill/threshold theme (#37, #49, #256, #48) is self-enabling via prepare mill spells; #49 becomes a 3/3 flier.
- #176 charm is flexible. Slower, so can fall behind fast red decks, but card advantage is real.

## WU — rank 5 (6.4)
- Surveil/fliers: #181 (flier + free 2/2 token), #162, #21 (premium 3/4 flier), #257, #171 charm.
- Good evasion with #13, #105, #108, #31; #223 and #161 as removal.
- Lacks hard removal beyond those; leans on tempo. Solid but not top.

## BG — rank 6 (6.0)
- Black removal carries it; green adds sturdy bodies (#114 5/5 ward, #124 4/4 flashback, #183) and #242 bite.
- Signposts #103 (removal on a body) and #23 are fine; #22 common aura is low-impact.
- Graveyard/land recursion theme is unfocused; no evasion besides menace.

## RG — rank 7 (5.7)
- Big green bodies + red removal is a reasonable shell; #78 (7/5 + 4 damage) and #172 are strong uncommons.
- Heartwood ramp (#47, #264, #168) is slow card-quality-wise; common #47 is a weak 2-drop.
- Little evasion and green lacks removal; vulnerable to fliers despite reach creatures.

## UR — rank 8 (5.6)
- Red removal is good, but spells-matter payoffs (#12, #68, #111, #59) need a noncreature count the commons don't supply.
- Gold common #50 is a marginal cantrip; #208 is a clunky removal.
- Creature quality is middling; deck often feels like two halves without synergy.

## WG — rank 9 (5.2)
- Lifegain/+1 counter theme (#170, #263, #67, #20, #177) is cute but the gold common #170 is a 1/1.
- White removal helps; green bodies are fine, but the pair lacks black/red's removal and has limited evasion beyond white fliers.
- #177 fight charm and #20 are the best gold pieces.

## UG — rank 10 (5.0)
- Jace "empower" ramp/value theme (#238, #204, #205, #207, #159) is slow and doesn't affect the board much.
- Removal is the thinnest of any pair (#161, #242, #96 fight); weak to removal-heavy BR/WB decks.
- #96 is a good top end, but overall the deck durdles in a removal-rich format.
